Privacy

Privacy statement

How we handle your data. Short, with no small print.

Who we are

Energie-Compas is a service of Fluctus.net CVSO, company number BE 0757 494 180. Questions about your data: dossier@energie-compas.eu.

Which data we use, and why

  • Your bill and metering data: consumption, injection, connection, EAN and quarter-hour values. We use them for your simulation and your report. If you give us a mandate, we retrieve your metering data from the grid operator.
  • Your contact details: name, e-mail, phone, address and company number. We use them to send you your results, to contact you and to link your file to your adviser.
  • Your vehicles and your location: which vehicles you charge and when, and where your connection is. We need these to calculate your peak, your charging cost and the grid infrastructure nearby.
  • Use of your results: when your results, documents or videos are opened and which buttons you use. This lets your adviser know whether you saw your results and help you at the right moment. We also use it to improve our screens. See below how we do this without cookies.
  • Website visits: which pages of energie-compas.eu are visited and from which source. Counts only, without cookies.

Counting without cookies

For the website statistics and the use of your results we set no cookies and store nothing in your browser. To count visits, our server creates a code each day from your IP address and browser type, with a key that changes every day and is not stored anywhere. That code is only recognisable within the same day and cannot be traced back to your IP address or device. For this counting we do not store your IP address. Only when you sign or accept a quote or contract online do we store your IP address and browser type, as proof of that signature.

So we count devices per day, not people: if you look on your phone and your laptop, that is two devices.

The only things kept in your browser are what the app needs to work: your sign-in and your settings, such as the language.

Who we share your data with

  • Your adviser, if your file has one. They see your results and how often they were opened.
  • Installers, only if you request a quote, and only what they need for that quote.
  • Service providers who help us: hosting and storage, e-mail delivery and map services (including OpenStreetMap, Esri and Google Maps) to put your address on the map.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

Legal basis

We use your data to do what you ask: calculate your bill and deliver your results. Counting visits and the use of your results is based on our legitimate interest: following up on your file properly and improving our service. You can object to this at any time.

How long we keep your data

We keep your file for as long as needed for your project and its follow-up. If you ask us to delete your file, we will. The website statistics consist only of daily counts.

Your rights

You can always ask which data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to counting the use of your results, or take your data with you. Write to dossier@energie-compas.eu. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).
