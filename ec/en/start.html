What you see — 2

Your grid connection can handle more than you think.

Charging doesn’t have to raise your busiest quarter-hour. During the day, the battery stores your solar power. At the busy moment, it gives it back. So you charge far more on the same connection, and a heavier connection often becomes unnecessary.
