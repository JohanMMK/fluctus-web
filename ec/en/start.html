The starting point

What holds back electrification is not technical.
It is uncertainty.

Companies want to electrify their mobility, but get stuck on two questions: what does charging cost per kilometre, and does my grid connection need an upgrade? Today you answer them with a costly study — or not at all.

Our experience from hundreds of cases: the solution does not lie in a heavier grid connection. It lies in two things you already have — your energy bills and your existing grid connection. That is where the headroom is. You simply need to learn to read them and make better use of them.

What you see — 2

Your existing grid connection can handle more than you think.

Electric charging does not have to raise your peak. Your solar panels and a battery do the work: during the day the battery absorbs the solar peak, and at busy moments it feeds the energy back. This way you charge far more than your peak kW while staying within the same contracted capacity — a costly grid upgrade becomes unnecessary.

Why the value keeps rising

Two forces push in the same direction. Your grid connection is becoming more expensive — regulated grid tariffs rise year after year and double from 2029, so every avoided peak is worth more tomorrow. And with a better-used grid connection, you buy more at the right moments — precisely when electricity is cheap or even negatively priced. The more volatile the market, the larger that margin.
